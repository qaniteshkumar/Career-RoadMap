About CareerMap Pro


CareerMap Pro is an interactive career discovery concept designed to help students move from career confusion to a structured learning journey.


Students can explore careers, complete a career-matching quiz, identify relevant skills, follow a roadmap and track progress.


Prototype note: The CareerMatch system in this demo runs locally in JavaScript. A production version could connect it to real AI, authentication, a database, verified courses and live career data.
