About CareerOrbit

CareerOrbit is an original interactive career-planning concept. It takes the familiar roadmap idea and adds a progression system, career discovery, skill constellation and actionable missions.

The current demo works entirely in the browser using JavaScript and localStorage. A production version could connect real authentication, a database, AI recommendations, verified learning resources and live job data.
